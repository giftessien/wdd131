Digital Skills for Everyone

Digital skills are open to everyone, irrespective of age, educational qualification, occupation, or previous digital experience. Don't be left behind in the digital world! Take the first step towards a brighter future by joining Digital Skills for Everyone.

Purpose

The ultimate goal of this programme is to bridge the digital skills gap and create opportunities for everyone to succeed in the digital age. Digital Skills for Everyone website is to provide a simple, accessible, and user-friendly platform where people can learn, develop, and improve their digital skills.

Target

Digital Skills for Everyone is designed for beginners, students, and individuals who want to improve their digital knowledge and understand basic web development.

Questions to deal with

  • How can I understand HTML, CSS, and JavaScript?
  • How can I protect my personal information online?
  • Where can I practise my digital skills?

Color Schema

  • Navy: #102A43 – header, footer and headings.
  • Blue: #2563EB – buttons and interactive links.
  • Cyan: #22D3EE – decorative accents.
  • Light: #F8FAFC – page background.
  • Dark text: #1E293B – body text.

Typography

  • Poppins – headings and titles.
  • Open Sans – paragraphs and general content.
  • Monospace – programming examples.

Structure

  • Home – introduction and featured learning topics.
  • Web Development – HTML, CSS and JavaScript lessons.
  • Digital Safety – online privacy and internet safety.
  • Quiz – interactive digital skills assessment.
  • References – sources of information and images.
